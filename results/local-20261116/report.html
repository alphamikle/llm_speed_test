Interpretation and limitations

One row aggregates one model, language and kit across repetitions. Step-level raw measurements are in steps.csv. Retries and repairs are measured. Screenplay pages are explicit A4 page breaks with minimum body text; semantic film quality is not certified. STE uses constrained prompting and a sentence-length audit, not a licensed dictionary certification. Browser games are exercised with real Chromium and external rules/AI tests. Native PP can be absent even when TTFT and output-token usage are available.
